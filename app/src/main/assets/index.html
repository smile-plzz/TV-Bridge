      Connection tips

      The bridge keeps running when you open a photo, switch apps, or press TV Home. Stop it on TV or from its notification. It automatically stops after two hours. Pairing codes last ten minutes; approved sessions last one hour.

      Keep this phone awake during uploads. If Safari pauses the page, choose the same file to resume. Guest Wi-Fi, VPNs and network isolation can prevent connection.

      Local HTTP is unencrypted. Use only on a trusted home network. No screen mirroring, system-wide remote, or private diagnostics from other apps.
