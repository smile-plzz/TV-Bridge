Shared on TV

    Failed or cancelled uploads can be retried from the start. Downloads are saved through your browser.

    Skip the remote typing.

    Links need approval on the TV and an installed app that can open them. Text appears in TV Bridge and can be copied; typing directly into other apps is not supported.

    Just between your devices.

    TV Bridge uses your local network. No cloud account or upload service.

    Includes TV Bridge version, device information, storage, and recent transfer events. Review before sharing. Other apps’ reports are not included.

    Connection tips

    Keep this app open on TV and your phone awake. Guest Wi-Fi, VPNs, and network isolation can prevent connection. If the TV address changes, scan its new QR code.

    Local HTTP is unencrypted. Use only on a trusted network. Pairing expires after 10 minutes; approved sessions last one hour. Leaving the TV app disconnects everyone.
