# Store listing draft

**Name:** TV Bridge

**Short description:** Share files, text and links between your phone browser and Android TV.

**Full description:**

TV Bridge brings your phone and Android TV together over your home network. Install the app on your TV, scan its QR code with your phone, enter the on-screen code and approve the connection using your remote.

Send photos, videos, documents and APK files to the TV. Download shared TV files through your phone browser. Search, filter and sort your shared library. Pause an upload and choose the same file to resume. Open a shared file on TV with confirmation, then use your phone to control videos playing inside TV Bridge.

The connection can keep running while you use other TV apps. Stop it on the TV or from its notification; it automatically stops after two hours. No phone app, account, cloud upload, advertising or analytics is needed.

Android TV 8.0+ and a modern phone browser are required. Both devices must share a trusted IPv4 home network. Local HTTP traffic is unencrypted. Uploads support files up to 1 GiB. Photos, video formats, document pickers and APK installation depend on your TV’s available Android capabilities. The app does not mirror screens, control other applications or access their private data.
